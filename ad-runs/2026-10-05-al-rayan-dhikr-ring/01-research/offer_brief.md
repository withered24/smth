## Potential UMP (unique mechanism of the problem)

* The problem is not the person's intention, it is **friction and attention**: counting dhikr by loud clicker, loose beads or phone app costs attention, makes noise or collects distractions; the count gets lost at interruptions (VOC-21, VOC-29, VOC-30). [user doc §Frustrations; VOC]
* Supportability: **supportable as market-voice and design logic** (VOC-based); **weak as a scientific claim** (no research on dhikr-tool friction was supplied or sought; do not cite studies). Frame as "what people tell us" not "research shows".
