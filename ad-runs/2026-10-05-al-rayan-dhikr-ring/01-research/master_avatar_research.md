## 3. Pains / desires / fears map
- **Pains:** losing count when interrupted (Q29); loud clicker in quiet rooms (Q21); juggling several counters (Q01);

- **Desires:** dhikr woven into the whole day — commute, desk, walk, night (Q18, Q20, Q22, Q39); seeing progress over

  finger counting is Sunnah (Q07, Q36); index-finger ring for men (Q28); cheap clones die (Q11); wrong size (Q26);
  looking odd at work (Q40).

premium iQibla ($149 | price, weight). Competitors: iQibla, Weslamic/iTasbih, Hadiyah Noor 2.0 ($49), Sajada.

## 5. Mechanism + beliefs
- **Big Idea:** "Blame your tools, not your intention" (matches live store headline). Dhikr was never weak — loud
  clickers, loose beads and a phone full of notifications made it hard.
- **UMP:** friction + attention — counting by clicker/beads/app makes noise, costs attention, loses the count at every
  interruption (Q21, Q29, Q30). Market voice, not science — never "research shows".
- **UMS:** a ring on the finger: physical tap → small digital count → gentle vibration at 33 / 66 / 99, so you know where
  you are without sound, without looking, without the phone; prayer-time alerts; app sync (product page).
- **Beliefs** (`necessary_beliefs.md`): B1 small constant dhikr all day matters (Tirmidhi 3375, Bukhari 6464) → B2 what
  stops me is friction, not intention → B3 counting aids are permitted, finger counting stays Sunnah (Abu Dawud 1501) →
  B4 a ring can count silently anywhere → B5 built for daily life, $49.99 fair, 60-day money-back → B6 trying it is
  low-risk and the easiest way to keep dhikr steady.
- **Verified faith lines** (offer_brief.md "Verified Islamic sources"): Quran 13:28, 2:152, 33:41, 33:35, 62:10, 3:191;
  Tirmidhi 3375; Abu Dawud 1501; Bukhari 6406, 6405, 6464; Muslim 596a (33/33/34 after prayer); Muslim 2702 (istighfar
  100/day). Rejected: Tirmidhi 3583, Muslim 783. Say what the dhikr earns, never what the ring earns.
- **Product facts usable:** $49.99, free US shipping, 60-day money-back, 1-year tech warranty, vibrations 33/66/99,
  prayer-time alerts (5 prayers), app sync, IP67 (no "waterproof for showers/swimming"), black + rose gold, display shows
  4 digits. NOT usable: store metrics/stars, 7.2 g, IP68, battery days, "completely silent" (say "silent tracking"),
  works-without-phone, size exchange, sale price (unconfirmed), delivery speed.
