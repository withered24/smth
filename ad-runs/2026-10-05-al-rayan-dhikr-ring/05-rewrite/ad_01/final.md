## Editor notes

### What I changed from the rewrite, and why
1. **"Lights off" removed from the hook.** The picture shows a lit lamp, so "Lights off. A few more SubhanAllah." contradicted the creative. New H1: "One lamp still on. A few more SubhanAllah." (matches the lamp, the mug and the hand). Also updated the "re-render" and "fallback" notes to say so.
2. **"We hear it often" cut** (hook H3 and body). It asserts customer feedback we cannot verify. Replaced with a plain observation, "A big dhikr week, then a quiet one: it happens", which keeps the burst-then-quiet idea with no claim and no shame. Body now reads as one reassurance sentence plus the gloss.
3. **"you feel a nudge at each of the five prayers" changed to "prayer-time alerts for the five daily prayers".** "Feel a nudge" implies a vibration on prayer alerts, which the allowed claims do not say. "Prayer-time alerts" is the allowed wording. "Your days are waiting in the app" became "your days in the Al Rayan app" (less of a claim about stored data). [CONFIRM] tag on the app kept.
4. **Product line merged and tightened.** The two separate "keeps the count on your hand" and "the count is already on it" statements repeated each other. Now one line: "tap to count, silent tracking, a gentle vibration at 33, 66 and 99." Dropped "keeps the count on your hand" from the body (it sounded like the ring stores your count across interruptions, which is unconfirmed).
5. **Close.** "Try it for 60 days." changed to "Start small." Ad_02, ad_05 and ad_11 all close on "try it for 60 days"; "Start small." is on-angle and unique. Meta headline 3 changed from "Try it 60 days. Money back." to "Free US shipping. 60-day money-back." (36) so it also differs from ad_09's "60 days, money back" headline and the CTA line.
6. **Meta description shortened** from about 61 characters to 44 ("Vibration at 33, 66, 99. Prayer-time alerts.").
7. **H4 hook rewritten** so the only quoted words are an exact substring of Bukhari 6464 ("the most regular and constant even if it were little"), with the Prophet ﷺ named outside the quote. H6 reworded to the allowed "prayer-time alerts" wording ("nudges you" dropped).
8. Word counts rechecked (135 body words without the tag, 149 with it). Headline counts: 37, 31, 36. All at or under 40. On-image caption 17 characters.
