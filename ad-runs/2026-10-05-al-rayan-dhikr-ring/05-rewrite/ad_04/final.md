## Editor notes

### What I changed from the rewrite, and why
1. **Meta headlines reworked.** The rewrite's "Dhikr on your finger. Nothing to scroll." (exactly 40 characters, no margin) put "dhikr" and "finger" together, which ad_01's editor deliberately avoided because it muddles with Sunnah finger counting. New headline 1 is the screen-versus-ring contrast (39 chars), which matches the picture (phone screen vs the ring's lit display) and reads as a visual, not a claim. Headline 2 swaps "finger" for "hand" (38). Headline 3 kept (30). Dropped "No feed on your finger." for the same finger reason.
2. **H6 replaced.** "We hear this a lot..." asserts customer feedback we cannot verify, and ad_01's editor cut the same device. New H6 is a plain observation. The old H5 became the contrast H4, and the "dhikr on your hand" line is now H5.
3. **Sunnah line reworded.** "The ring is for the long counts and busy hands" was near-identical to ad_08 ("a helper for long counts and busy hands") and close to ad_06 and ad_01. New line: "Counting on the fingers is the Sunnah. The ring is there when the count runs into the hundreds." Same respect, no ranking above finger counting, distinct wording.
4. **Mechanism tightened.** "tells you where you are without looking down" over-claimed (the pulse only fires at 33, 66, 99, and you look at the display for the number). Now: "The number shows on the ring's own small display. A gentle pulse at 33, 66 and 99 marks your place." Accurate, does not imply the 100th, ties to the display visible in the picture.
5. **CONFIRM scoped narrowly.** "Nothing to scroll." is true of any ring (no feed) and needs no tag. Only "Nothing to unlock." depends on the unconfirmed works-without-your-phone claim, so the tag now sits on that one sentence alone and can be deleted cleanly.
6. **Phone line tightened.** "A tasbih app is a fine tool. It just lives on the same screen as the feed, so a quick count can turn into forty minutes." Cut "opening it for one count" (clunky), hedged ("can turn into"), no app-bashing, no assertion about the viewer.
7. **On-image headline status clarified.** The rewrite listed the rendered second-person headline as "kept" with the neutral version optional. For Meta personal-attributes safety at the most visible spot in the feed, the neutral version ("the phone", not "your phone") is now the primary listed version, with the rendered text as fallback. Same eight words, same layout.
