## Editor notes

### What I changed from the rewrite, and why
1. **Hook "Istighfar, all the way to work." became "Istighfar, the whole walk to work."** This ad is the batch's only walk, and "walk" matches the sidewalk on screen (the commute is a drive in the research). Same strengths: tribe word first, no "you", no mental state.
2. **C01 pill "ONE TAP. ONE COUNT." became "MORNING WALK".** C01 shows no tap, so the pill promised something the picture does not. The new pill is a scene label (morning light is on screen) and adds a beat the VO does not say. "ONE TAP, ONE COUNT" moved to C02, where a ring and display are on screen.
3. **Cut every "eyes up / eyes on the street" line** (the old H5, the primary-text "lets you keep your eyes on the street"). ad_05's editor cut the same claim as an overclaim (you still look at the display to read the count) and flagged it as an echo of this ad.
4. **Cut "SILENT TRACKING" as a C02 overlay.** ad_06's VO says "Silent tracking", so it would have been a repeat. The brand phrase stays in the primary text, once.
5. **Verse and its attribution tightened.** Quran 62:10 is spoken as "The Quran says: remember Allah often." That is an exact substring of the verified wording (row 5), attributed aloud and on screen. The rewrite's VO inserted a comma and ended on "that you may succeed", which sat two lines after "Tap to count" and let "succeed" read as an outcome of the ring. The VO now stops at "often" and runs slower (2.1 w/s, down from the rewrite's 2.5). The full contiguous clause, with the verified wording untouched, stays in the primary text, placed before any product sentence.
6. **Bukhari 6464 removed from the primary text.** ad_01 owns it (and quotes it with the correct "The Prophet ﷺ said (narrated by Aisha)" attribution). Using it a second time made two ads share a faith line. B1 is now carried by "remember Allah often" plus the Sunnah line, and I did not claim more than that. The Sunnah line was reworded ("This ring is a helper for the long walk and the long count") because "helper for long counts and busy hands" had already been flagged as near-identical across ad_01, ad_04, ad_06 and ad_11.
7. **Dropped the "[CONFIRM: works without your phone / app is optional]" sentence from the primary text.** Nothing in the ad depends on it, so there is nothing for the owner to confirm. No [CONFIRM] tag remains.
8. **Primary text product line.** Removed "lets you keep your eyes on the street" and ad_07's phrase "marks your place". Added "IP67 water resistant" (allowed; fine for rain on a walk) and nothing else.
9. **Headlines.** Old #3 "Dhikr count on your finger. Eyes up." used "eyes up" and "count on your finger" (ad_01 and ad_10 phrasing). Replaced with "One tap per istighfar. Keep walking." (37). All three are 40 characters or fewer.
10. **Meta description changed** to "$49.99 · Free US shipping" (25). The rewrite's version restated the same two offer items as the primary text; this one avoids a repeat of the guarantee line that ad_02, ad_06 and ad_07 already use in that slot.
11. **Hooks.** Dropped the "We hear it a lot" brand hook (implies customer reports we cannot source). Added H5 and H6. No first person anywhere.
12. **Captions.** Re-chunked to 14 characters or fewer and re-timed to the new line lengths. Added a note on the end card.
