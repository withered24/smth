# ad_05 critique (COPY CRITIC) — "Beads scatter. Clickers shout. This doesn't."

Ad: IMAGE 9:16, comparison (beads vs clicker vs ring bands), A1, ANG8, solution-aware, stage 3-4.
Copy reviewed: `03-ads/ad_05/copy_final.md` (identical to chiefed v2).
Creative reviewed: `report_media/ad_05_main.jpg` (cream, ring band last), `ad_05_img_v2.jpg` (ring band first), `ad_05_img_v3.jpg` (dark green, side-by-side rows).
Also read: CONTEXT.md, intake, user market research (incl. VOC table), avatar sheet, master avatar research, necessary beliefs, offer brief, brief.md, chief notes v1/v2, `_assign.json` entry, concept slate card C-02, the other 10 ads' copy_final.md, and the rebuilt `product.html` / `index.html`.

Bottom line: the previous chief approved this at "Tier 1, no residual concerns". That bar was mostly mechanical (word counts, banned-word scans). Read the way a senior DR copy chief with Islamic adab and Meta policy in mind would read it, this is a competent but generic comparison ad. The best element is the photo of the ring. The weakest elements are the second-person "you lost / you winced / weakness in you" lines, a hook whose payoff ("This doesn't.") has no antecedent, near-total overlap with ad_09, and a ring photo that appears to show the ring on the index/middle finger of a male-reading hand. Overall: 5.5/10 as written. It is fixable without changing the concept.

---

## 1. Hook strength (would A1 or A2 stop?)

How the hook works in the feed: the on-image headline and primary-text line 1 are the same words, so the hook is "Beads scatter. Clickers shout. This doesn't." plus a 3-band photo.

- What works: three beats, parallel verbs, 6 words. "Shout" is a good, physical word for a loud clicker. The three objects are instantly familiar, and the viewer has owned at least one.
- What does not: "This doesn't." has no concrete antecedent. It gets its meaning from the third band, which the thumb has to scroll past to read. In the main render, the headline carries no tribe marker. The words "dhikr", "tasbih", "istighfar", "salah" appear nowhere on the image. To a cold A1 it reads as a generic "counter ring vs. tally clicker" ad. The only faith cue is a string of wooden beads.
- "Beads scatter" is not the A1 pain. Q29 is losing your place when interrupted (kids), not beads exploding across a desk. That happens only when a string snaps, which is rare. The image then shows a tidy bundle plus 5 loose beads, so the premise reads as staged. A fiqh/tasbih-literate commenter will say "my misbaha has never scattered".
- A2 (gift buyer): nothing here speaks to them. The hook never names the category (a smart tasbih ring), and A2 is problem-aware, so it would not stop them. That is acceptable only if A2 is not a target here (brief says A1), so it is not a defect.

Ranking of the six existing hooks (best to worst, for A1 in a feed):

| Rank | Hook | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | H1 "Beads scatter. Clickers shout. This doesn't." | Only one that fits the 3-band image, good rhythm. Weak as a standalone line and the "scatter" claim is exaggerated. Keep only if the image carries the product and a dhikr cue. |
| 2 | H4 "Clicker echoing in your quiet office?" | Specific scene, situation-framed. "Your office" assumes a workplace but not a religion, so it is policy-safe. Strongest scroll-stop on its own. But it is also ad_09's and ad_11's territory. |
| 3 | H3 "One bump, and the count is gone." | Vivid micro-scene, but the bump-scatter premise has the same accuracy problem as H1. |
| 4 | H6 "Beads drop. Clickers click. Rings tap." | Rhymey. "Clickers click / rings tap" is a distinction without a difference to the reader. |
| 5 | H5 "Still losing count?" | Generic. Duplicates ad_10 H5 "Lost count again?" and H1 "Lost your dhikr count?". |
| 6 | H2 "I lost my count on physical beads." | First-person VOC reads as a customer testimonial (fabricated-endorsement risk per CONTEXT). Weak. Do not use. |

## 2. Angle, avatar fit, awareness/sophistication, belief order

- Angle (old tools fail) fits A1's real frictions (Q21 loud clicker, Q29 lost count). It is a legitimate angle.
- Sophistication mismatch. The brief says stage 3-4, "mechanism stage, with copied mechanisms". Everyone claims lightweight/OLED/app/waterproof. The ad compares the ring only to beads and clickers, tools the A1 researcher has already moved past. A1 is comparing rings (iQibla, Hadiyah, clones) and fears "cheap clone dies in a month" (Q11, listed in this ad's own brief VOC and then not used anywhere). Nothing in the ad answers "why this ring and not another smart ring". Allowed differentiators that go unused: 1-year tech warranty, 60 days to try, the small display showing the count, IP67, app sync.
- The mechanism is buried. The pulse at 33/66/99 is the one concrete mechanism and it appears only in the Meta headline and primary line 6. The original concept card had "Ring: tap. Pulse at 33." The final dropped it, leaving the winning band with the least specific label ("silent tap").
- Belief order. Brief lists B4, B2. Copy installs B2 (L1, L4) then B4 (L5-6) — fine in that order. But:
  - B2 is installed by assertion ("Your intention was never the problem.", "That is friction in the tool, not weakness in you."), not by a scene. It does the opposite of what the research says to do (walk the failure in the avatar's own scene).
  - B4 "a ring can count silently, without a phone, noise or a look at a screen, so I can keep going wherever I am" is only half-installed: silent yes, "without a look at a screen / wherever I am" no. The pulse is what delivers "without looking down", and it is the least developed line.
  - B3 is deliberately skipped. Defensible given the brief, but see the faith/adab note in section 3: this comparison attacks beads, and the Sunnah-respect line is the guard against that.
  - B5/B6: offer line and 60-day guarantee are present. Fine.
- N4 from the chief (an earned identity line for A1) was never applied. The ad has no moment (meeting, commute, bedside), only objects.

## 4. Copy-to-creative mismatch and on-image text

What the image shows (main render): cream background; headline top; three bands. (1) A tidy wooden bead strand with 5 loose beads and a frayed cord, labelled with a red X "Beads: count gone". (2) A metal hand tally clicker with three sound arcs, labelled "Clicker: everyone hears". (3) A hand on a laptop keyboard wearing the rose-gold ring, display reading 5332, labelled with a green check "Ring: silent tap". The bottom ~15% is empty cream.

Mismatches and problems:
- Third band shows typing, not tapping. The hand is on the keys, the thumb is nowhere near the side button. The ring looks like jewelry; the thing the copy promises ("Tap to count", "gentle vibration at 33, 66 and 99") is not shown, and neither is any pulse. The visual shows "ring on a typing hand", not "ring counts".
- Beads band does not show "count gone" or "scatter". It shows a mostly intact bracelet-style strand. The label has to do work the picture does not.
- No dhikr/tasbih/Al Rayan cue anywhere on the image, not even the product name. The gold logo button is tiny. The ad does not say what the ring is for.
- Right-hand/left-hand ambiguity and the finger issue (section 3 #7).
- Dead space at the bottom, while the three labels are small and thin relative to the 9:16 frame. v3 (green) fixes some of this (stronger contrast and hierarchy) but the labels are still small. If a re-render is happening, v3's dark green is more on-brand for the audience and reads better at thumb size than the cream version.
- Label quality: colon-fragments ("Beads: count gone", "Clicker: everyone hears", "Ring: silent tap") are tidy but flat. The third label is the weakest, the others show a failure and the third shows a vague virtue. The concept card's own "Ring: tap. Pulse at 33." was better.
- Copy-to-image redundancy: primary L1 repeats the on-image headline word for word. The primary text should extend the image, not echo it.

Re-render needs (flag to the orchestrator): (a) ring on ring finger or neutral/female hand, (b) thumb on the button mid-tap, (c) add "dhikr" somewhere, (d) possibly new label 3 with the pulse, (e) fill or crop the bottom dead space. Text-only changes (headline/labels) still require a re-render, so only do them if the changes are the (c)/(d) ones, which are clearly worth it.

2. "No click. No beads. Just tap." — closest to the angle; "No beads" is the knock again, and it is a near-duplicate of ad_09 H5 "No click. No beads." and ad_11 "No loud clicker. No beads to drop. No phone to open."
3. "$49.99. Free US shipping." — fine as a pure-offer alt.

Meta description "60-day money-back guarantee" duplicates primary L7.

Two feed ads with the same claim, same VOC, same offer, running to the same audience will compete and look repetitive.

- "Lost count" in ad_01, 02, 07, 10.

## MUST FIX

1. Remove "weakness in you" (L4) and the accusatory second-person lines L2/L3 ("You lost your count... / You winced..."). Replace with scene or product framing that never tells the reader what they did, felt or are. Also drop "Your intention was never the problem." from this ad (used in 4 others, and a niyyah assertion).
2. Visual: ring must not be shown on the index or middle finger of a male-reading hand (all three renders). Re-render on the ring finger, ideally mid-tap. Tell the owner the landing page FAQ ("Most people wear it on the index finger") needs the same fix.
3. Break the near-duplicate with ad_09: change the claim, the villain order or the frame so the two ads are not the same ad.
4. Primary text must not just echo the on-image headline in L1. Image headline and primary line 1 should do different jobs.
5. Do not use H2 (first-person VOC) as a hook, and keep every VOC phrase out of quote marks and out of first person.
6. "Ring: silent tap" -> brand wording ("silent tracking" / "quiet tap"). Any "count stays on the ring", "no phone needed", or battery/size-exchange line must carry a [CONFIRM] tag.

## SHOULD FIX

1. Put a dhikr/tasbih cue on the image (headline or label) and, if it can be done cheaply, the pulse at 33 on label 3 ("Ring: tap, pulse at 33").
2. Show the tap (thumb on button) and, if possible, the pulse, not a typing hand.
3. Soften the beads knock. "Beads scatter" is not the real pain (lost place when interrupted is) and invites misbaha-defence in comments. Either reframe to "where each tool works" or aim the criticism at the situation, not the heritage object.
4. Replace "It looks like a ring." with something specific and visible (the small display shows the count / gold Al Rayan logo button).
5. Use the unused VOC and benefits: Q11 (clone fear) is answered by 1-year tech warranty + 60-day money-back; Q32 (tech jewelry, not a toy) by the look; Q26 (size) by the 16-22 mm sizes.
6. Replace the Meta headline with one that carries the angle or a concrete benefit and stays within 40 characters. "Gentle vibrations at 33, 66 and 99" is a spec.
7. Drop "friction" as a word. The avatar says "tools", "loud", "lost count".
8. Cut or recast "Tap Shop Now and try it." ("try it" should point at the 60 days) and make the description line say something different from primary L7.
9. Fill the empty bottom 15% of the frame or crop the 9:16 so the labels can be larger.

## KEEP (what works)

- The three-object comparison idea. Instantly legible, and it is the format that gives this ad its own look versus the other ten.
- The verbs "scatter"/"shout" as a rhythm (even if "scatter" needs a truer scene).
- The ✓/✗ grammar and the ring-last placement (with the ring as the hero).
- "Silent tracking, so there is no click to explain." (human, specific, brand-wording-safe).
- "A gentle vibration at 33, 66 and 99 tells you where you are." — strongest sentence in the ad.
- Clean offer line with only allowed facts ($49.99, free US shipping, 60-day money-back).
- Absence of faith misquotes, metrics, stars, competitor names, sale price and "waterproof". Do not lose this.
- Category-level objects only; finger counting is never shown or knocked.

## Directions for the rewrite (3-5)

1. Scene instead of accusation. Open the primary text with a situation the avatar recognises (a quiet room, the kids walking in mid-count, a clicker out on the desk) in neutral or "we hear this a lot" voice, never "You lost... / You winced...". Let the reader place themselves. Example lead: "Mid-count, the kids walk in. Where were you?" or "A quiet meeting. A clicker is not an option." No claims about the reader's inner state.
2. Honest comparison frame, "every tool has its place". Beads are beautiful at home, the clicker is fine in a car, the ring is for the moments they cannot go. This matches the rebuilt landing page voice, respects the misbaha and the Sunnah (optional single line: "Fingers remain the Sunnah. The ring is for the long counts."), pre-empts the fiqh commenters, and clearly differs from ad_09's "RETIRE THE LOUD CLICKER" aggression.
3. Make the ring band the answer to the sophisticated A1: tap, a small display showing the count, a pulse at 33/66/99 so you do not look down, and the "built to be kept" proof that is allowed (1-year tech warranty, 60-day money-back, $49.99). This answers Q11 (cheap clone dies) without naming a competitor.
4. Fix the creative where it is clearly worth a re-render: ring on the ring finger, thumb on the button, a dhikr cue on the image, and the third label upgraded to "Ring: tap, pulse at 33" (or "quiet tap"). Keep text changes to those; do not redesign the layout. v3 (dark green) is the stronger base.
5. Differentiate from the set: ad_05 owns "the three tools, side by side, honestly" (not "your tools are to blame" and not the 33/66/99 feature list). Use a short, plain primary text (about 5 lines), one CTA, one offer line, and a Meta headline that names a benefit or the comparison ("Beads, clicker or ring?" style) rather than a spec. Avoid the shared phrases "your intention", "your dhikr was never weak", "no beads to drop" and "Tap Shop Now".
