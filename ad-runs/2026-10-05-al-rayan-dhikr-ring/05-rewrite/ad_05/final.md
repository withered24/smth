## Editor notes

### What changed from the rewrite, and why
1. **On-image headline "Which one comes to the meeting?" → "Which one fits a quiet room?"** Meeting/lecture framing was flagged elsewhere in this round as a divided-attention adab issue (ad_06, ad_11) and "meeting" is the scene ad_06 and ad_09 H3 own. "Quiet room" keeps the question, answers the clicker contrast, and assumes no workplace. The image is being re-rendered anyway, so no extra cost.
2. **Primary line 1 rewritten.** The rewrite opened "A quiet room. A clicker would turn every head." That is the same scene as ad_09 H3 ("The meeting goes quiet. The clicker doesn't.") and makes the clicker the villain again. New line 1 is the honest role split ("Beads suit the prayer mat. A clicker suits an empty room. A smart tasbih ring suits the rest of your day."): it answers the image question, names the category before "See more", and the three-beat is role-based, not the object list ad_02 uses.
3. **Removed "lecture, waiting room or meeting"** (ad_11 owns the waiting room; meeting/lecture carries the adab flag). Scenes are now generic ("quiet room", "rest of your day").
4. **Sunnah guard line reworded** from "counting on the fingers stays the Sunnah. The ring is a helper..." (near-identical to ad_01 and ad_10) to "It doesn't replace the beads, and fingers remain the Sunnah. It's one more tool, for the long counts." Keeps the ring below finger counting, no "busy hands" (ad_10).
5. **Cut "lets you keep your eyes up".** It overclaims (you still look at the display for the exact count) and echoes ad_08 ("EYES UP / eyes on the street"). Now: the display shows where you are, the vibration at 33, 66 and 99 "marks the way". No implication that the ring marks a 100th.
6. **"Built to be kept" → "Backed by a 1-year tech warranty and a 60-day money-back guarantee."** "Built to be kept" is an unprovable durability claim; the warranty and guarantee are the allowed facts and answer the clone-dies fear (Q11). Offer line uses the exact "60-day money-back guarantee" wording.
7. **CTA "Shop Now and try it for 60 days." → "Shop Now."** "Try it for 60 days" is now used in ad_09 and ad_11; 60 days already appears once in the offer line.
8. **Meta headlines 2 and 3 changed.** Old #2 "Built to be kept. 60 days to try." echoed the durability claim and ad_09 H6 / ad_02 headline territory; old #3 "The ring for the quiet room" is now headline 2 as "A smart tasbih ring for quiet rooms" (names the category, 35 chars). New #3 "60 days to try. 1-year warranty." (32).
9. **Hooks table trimmed to five.** Dropped H6 "The tasbih you can use in a lecture hall" (lecture scene, adab flag) and the old H4 "Built to be kept" hook.
10. **Kept from the rewrite:** all on-image labels and the subhead (dhikr cue), "Silent tracking, so there is no click to explain" (brand wording), "Beads: lovely at home" (adab-safe), and all render notes.
