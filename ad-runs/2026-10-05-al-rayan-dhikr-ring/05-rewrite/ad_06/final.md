## Editor notes

### What I changed from the rewrite, and why
1. L2 line 3: "Feel a pulse at thirty-three" became "Silent tracking." Three reasons. (a) At 1.7 s of window it was 6 words, about 3.5 w/s, too fast to land. (b) ad_07 owns the "pulse at 33" demo (its H5, headline and VO), so this would have been a near-duplicate line across two video ads. (c) Over the shipped picture, which shows a glow on the band, "pulse" invites a light-equals-vibration misread. "Silent tracking" is the brand's own wording and fits this ad's whole premise (the room hears nothing). The 33, 66 and 99 vibration stays in the primary text.
2. L2 re-timed to the measured picture. The other finger arrives at about 3.65 s and touches at about 4.4-4.7 s, with the glow running from about 4.7 s. The rewrite put "Tap to count dhikr" at 3.2-4.5, before the finger appeared. Now "Tap" starts at 3.45 and "dhikr" lands on the touch.
3. L3 trimmed and re-timed. The rewrite spoke "Free US shipping. Sixty-day money-back." (about 5 tokens that are 11 syllables) in 2.2 s, which is a rushed close. VO now says only the guarantee; the baked pill carries free shipping, so each offer is stated once and neither contradicts the other. The payoff line now runs 7.25-9.00 into the smile, which I measured as starting at about 8.75 s (the rewrite assumed 8.0-9.4).
4. Hooks H2, H4, H5 replaced. Old H2 "Looks like a ring. Counts dhikr." was a near-copy of ad_11's alt hook "Looks like a ring. Counts like a tasbih." Old H4 "Your dhikr doesn't have to stop" asserted the viewer's practice (borderline personal attribute); now "The dhikr". Old H5 "passes for jewellery" implied concealment; now "you wear like a ring".
5. Primary text rebuilt for uniqueness and compliance. New opener; product name moved inside the first 125 characters; the hadith gets a lead-in ("Dhikr is much older than any ring") so it cannot read as the ring fulfilling the hadith; the Sunnah line is reworded because "Fingers stay the Sunnah. The ring is for the long counts." is verbatim ad_09's H5 and close to ad_05, ad_07 and ad_11. "Across a meeting table, it looks like a ring" sat too close to ad_11's "In the waiting room, it just looks like a ring", so the line now frames the glance and the display up close.
6. Burned captions re-chunked to 14 characters or fewer; the rewrite's "Tap to count dhikr", "Dhikr in the quiet gaps" and "Sixty-day money-back" would wrap to two lines at 720 px width.
7. Meta description cut to "60-day money-back guarantee" (27) so it neither repeats the headline nor the pill, and stays short for the link-description slot.
8. Caption position: kept the rewrite's `MarginV 540` for C01/C03 but dropped the change for C02, so the caption does not jump around the finger and glow zone.
