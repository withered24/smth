### Clip change notes
All of the script above works on the clips exactly as they are. Nothing is required. Ranked optional changes, in order of value:
1. **C02 (recommended re-roll):** v1 shows a white flare on the band as the "vibration". The product vibrates; it does not flash. In the current script the words never claim a flash ("feel a pulse"), but the picture still shows one at about 3.3-4.5 s. Re-roll C02 with (a) a same-hand thumb tap (the product is tapped, and a two-handed poke undercuts "discreet"), (b) no flash, or only a barely visible display glow, (c) a legible count that visibly rises (v1 digits render as garbled "2EE5" instead of 5332). Keep the 4.7 s length and the pill text.
2. **C01 prompt:** v1 wanted the colleague to give "a small approving nod", which kills the joke. Re-roll with a puzzled half-glance and look-away, and slightly more hand motion (a pen tap) so the hook frame has something to watch. Keep the pill.
3. **C03 pill (optional):** FREE US SHIPPING now agrees with the VO, so it can stay. If a cleaner hierarchy is wanted, change the pill to "60-DAY MONEY-BACK" and speak free shipping only in the primary text. Not needed for this script.
4. **C01 pill (low priority):** the baked "WHAT MY COWORKERS SEE" is a meme caption in first person. It is not a testimony, but if the owner wants zero first person, swap it for "WHAT THE ROOM SEES".
5. **Honesty note on the picture:** C01 turns the display to the palm so the ring reads as a plain band. That is why the copy says "looks like a ring" and never "nobody can tell / nobody notices". C03 shows the lit display next to a pen, which is accurate. Do not extend the claim.
