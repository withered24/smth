Role: copy critic. Not a rewrite. Reviewed: `03-ads/ad_06/copy_final.md`, `brief.md`, chief notes, `04-assets/ad_06/script_display.txt`, `prompts/vo.txt`, `captions.ass`, `captions.words.json` (real VO timings), `clip_args.txt`, the 3 start frames, `final.mp4` (1 fps contact sheet), the swipe analysis (ErgoSalah 71735717), the other 10 ads' copy_final.md, and the rebuilt store pages.

Verdict in one line: the concept (meme inversion, hijabi woman in a meeting, ring as camouflage) is sound and the copy is clean on faith and metrics. But the hook is soft, the script is a feature list with a coupon at the end, the on-screen text and the VO disagree in two places, the first-person voice is a testimonial risk, and the angle is now nearly a duplicate of ad_11.

## 1. Hook strength and ranking

First second as shipped: a calm woman at a table, a colleague glance, a green pill, and a fast VO line. There is no movement, no tension, no visible object of interest (the ring is a plain black band with its display turned away, so there is literally nothing to look at), and no in-group flag in words. The swipe's hook (ErgoSalah) worked because the caption named the tribe ("What muslim think i sell" plus emoji) and the contrast was funny (🤢💀 vs 🥺☁️). Ours is a mild contrast (a person fidgeting vs a tasbih) with no word that tells the A1 scroller "this is for me". "Fidgeting" also invites the wrong read: a fidget-ring product.

Would A1/A2 stop? A1: maybe 50/50, mainly because of the hijab and "tasbih" arriving at second 2.4. A2 (gift buyer): no, nothing signals product category or gifting. The line is passive, observational, and gives no reason to stay for the reveal.

Ranking of the six hooks:
1. **H5 "Guess what I'm counting in this meeting." / NOT THE MINUTES.** Best curiosity gap, ties straight to the scene, the overlay joke is the funniest line in the document, and the reveal ("dhikr") is a real payoff. Risk: first person (see section 3). Reframe as POV caption or brand voice.
2. **H1 "Coworkers think I'm fidgeting with a ring."** The right swipe structure and a real moment (everyone has been the person with something in their hands in a meeting). Weakness: no in-group word, no stakes, rushed delivery. Needs a sharper first noun.
3. **H3 "Loud clicker. Quiet office. Everyone staring."** Strong VOC-21 pain and rhythm, but it is the ad_09 and ad_05 territory and nothing in the clips shows a clicker, so it would mismatch the picture.
4. **H6 "Modern tech jewelry, not a cheap plastic toy."** This is a lightly reworded Q32 review line turned into a brand claim, and "not a cheap plastic toy" implies a competitor comparison. Weak hook, no scene.
5. **H2 "I was afraid a tasbih ring would look ridiculous at work."** Q40 is a review of a different product, lifted into first person. Reads as a testimonial. Opening with a fear is also on the shaming side.
6. **H4 "Muslim at work? Your tasbih can look like a ring." / FOR MUSLIMS AT WORK.** Disqualified: asserts religion of the viewer (Meta personal-attributes) and presumes the viewer owns a tasbih.

## 2. Angle, avatar, awareness, belief order

- **Angle fit: good.** "Looks like an ordinary ring, works as a silent tasbih" is on-brief for A1 and backed by VOC-40/22/21 and the store ("To everyone else it's simply a ring").
- **Awareness/sophistication:** solution-aware, stage 3-4. The script skips straight to the product, which is right for A1, but at stage 3-4 the market has heard "silent, vibration, app, IP67" from every competitor. The script's only mechanism is those two feature words. It lacks a specific moment/mechanism (what the tap does, what you feel at 33) and an identification beat (why this matters in this room).
- **B4 is asserted, not demonstrated.** B4 = "a ring I wear can count silently, without a phone, noise or a look at a screen". The VO says "silent tracking, gentle vibration": two nouns, no verb, no benefit. The picture never shows a count rising (the display digits in the AI render are garbled/mirrored and not legible, roughly "2EE5" instead of 5332), no phone-free, no "you don't need to look". The pulse is shown as a bright white lens-flare on the band, which is not how a vibration works.
- **Beat B4 -> B6 is a jump.** B6 ("trying it is low-risk") is installed by a bare "Sixty days, money back." with no price, no reason to want it, and no payoff for the emotion the last clip is built around (her private smile). The brief's own optional faith payoff ("keep your tongue moist") was never used in the video, only in primary text. The emotional peak of the creative (C03, the half-smile) is wasted on an offer line.
- **"Tasbih" is the only identity word and it is never explained** ("It's my tasbih"). Fine for A1, opaque for A2, and "dhikr" never appears in VO, captions or pills. The ad never says what the ring does for a tasbih in plain words: it counts.
- **"Tracking" is the wrong verb** for dhikr audiences. The user doc itself says dhikr "was never a score". "Silent tracking" is allowed brand wording but "count" is warmer and the verb the avatar uses.

## 4. Copy-to-creative mismatch and on-image text

1. **Pill 3 "FREE US SHIPPING" vs VO "Sixty days, money back."** The pill is on screen for the full 4.5 s of C03 while the VO says a different offer. Free shipping is never spoken, and the guarantee is never on screen except as a caption. Two offers, no hierarchy. (Pill is baked into the clip start frame, so changing it means a clip change.)
2. **Pill 1 + caption + VO say the same thing three times** ("WHAT MY COWORKERS SEE", "Coworkers think I'm", "Coworkers think I'm fidgeting..."). Zero information added after the first word. Burned captions in C01 also sit directly on the hand and ring (the picture composes the hand low, around 70-85% of frame height, not at the 46-54% in the prompt), so the caption covers the subject of the shot.
3. **Pill 2 "VS... WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS" does not say what it is.** The reveal is then the VO "It's my tasbih", with no mention of dhikr or counting. A hard-cut reveal that withholds the payoff noun.
4. **Hook text says "fidgeting", the picture shows her perfectly still**, tapping a pen once. The "coworker unbothered/approving nod" in the prompt also undermines the comedy: if he is not puzzled, there is nothing to invert.
5. **The count never visibly rises** in C02, and the digits are garbled/mirrored. B4 and "tap to count" rely on the audience believing it.
6. **Tap mechanism shown is the other hand's index finger poking the ring.** The store describes a side button pressed with the thumb of the same hand. The shipped clip (c02_a2) was a fallback; it looks like a two-handed gesture in a "discreet" ad.
7. **C03: her mouth is slightly open in the first frame (t=7)** while the VO is not in sync, then she smiles. Reads as lip-sync noise. The smile is an emotional peak with no line under it.
8. **Pronunciation:** the brief says "TAZ-bee", the TTS prompt used "tas-beeh". Whisper confidence on "tasbih" was 0.09. Verify by ear; "tas-BEEH" is the community pronunciation.
9. **Typography:** pills are clean, correctly spelled, high contrast (good, keeps meme feel). Burned captions are Arial Black white with shadow, readable. Fine.

## 5. Clarity, rhythm, flab, overlap

- **Rhythm:** three separate mini-statements with silence between each, so it plays as slides, not as a flow. L2 is two nouns. L3 is a coupon read aloud ("Tap Shop Now" is an imperative with no reason).
- **"Tap Shop Now"** appears in the VO, the caption, and the Meta button. Same words in all 10 ads. Cliché, and in-video it is not clickable.
- **Primary text:** five lines, all generic: "Loud clickers and phone apps were never made for a quiet office." duplicates ad_09 ("Retire the loud clicker. It was never built for a quiet office") and ad_05; "Tap to count. A small display shows where you are. A gentle vibration at 33, 66 and 99." is a verbatim twin of ad_11, ad_10, ad_05, ad_07.
- **Direct overlap with ad_11 (image):** ad_11 hook "A ring with a hidden tasbih", ad_11 primary "It looks like a ring. Under the table, it counts your dhikr.", ad_11 headline "A tasbih hidden on your finger" vs ad_06 "Coworkers think it's just a ring. It's my tasbih, counting dhikr..." and headline "A tasbih that looks like a ring". Same angle, same scene (office/under the table), same reveal. Also overlaps ad_03 ("It looks like a ring, not a gadget"), ad_02 ("A discreet tool you can have all the time at work"), ad_09/ad_05 (quiet office, loud clicker). ad_06 needs its own territory: the social moment and the human reaction (how others read you), humour, the private smile. Not the feature.
- **Headlines:** "A tasbih that looks like a ring" (31) is a near duplicate of ad_11's. "Silent dhikr, even in meetings" is better but still generic. "Dhikr ring. 60-day money-back" is a label. The Meta description duplicates the headline 3.
- **Flab:** "WHAT MY COWORKERS SEE" repeated; "gentle vibration" without purpose; "through the whole meeting"; the primary text could lose lines 2 and 3 entirely.

## MUST FIX
1. Remove first-person testimony voice (L1, L2, primary text line 1/2, H2, H5 as "I"): rewrite as brand voice, a second-person relatable scenario, or POV captions. No "my tasbih".
2. Delete H4 and "FOR MUSLIMS AT WORK"; do not recreate any "Muslim at work / as a Muslim" framing. Prefer situation framing ("In the meeting"), not identity.
3. Fix the hook: first noun must carry tension and an in-group cue within about 2 s; speak it at a pace that lands (at most 5 words in 2.1 s).
4. Make the VO and on-screen offer agree (pill says FREE US SHIPPING, VO says 60 days). Either speak both in a clean order or request a pill change as a clip change.
5. Name the payoff in the reveal: say "dhikr" or "counts" in the VO or caption. Do not rely on "tasbih" alone.
6. Stop depicting a non-existent light flash as the vibration; fix wording or flag a clip change. Do not claim "nobody can tell / nobody notices".

## SHOULD FIX
- Replace "tracking" with "count" wherever the brand-wording constraint allows (keep "silent tracking" only where needed).
- Rework "counting dhikr through the whole meeting" (adab and professionalism).
- Give C03's smile a line; end on a human beat, then the offer. Add price ($49.99) or free shipping consistently, not both fragmentary.
- Replace "Tap Shop Now" in the VO with a reason or drop it from the VO (keep the Meta button).
- Differentiate the headlines and primary text from ad_11, ad_09 and ad_05; remove the verbatim "Tap to count. A small display shows where you are..." paragraph.
- Move burned captions off the ring and hand in C01 and C03.
- Cut the hadith from where it implies the ring fulfils it, or give it a one-line lead-in, keep exact wording and "The Prophet ﷺ said" attribution.
- Verify "tasbih" pronunciation in the audio.
- Flag the store testimonials and 8,200+ metrics to the owner.

## KEEP (what works)
- The inversion structure and the fact that there is a person, a room and a reaction (the colleague glance) in the first frame.
- The hijabi persona and the modest, believable office; woman on the ring finger (no men's finger issue).
- Pill typography and the meme device: clean, spelled right, high contrast. The Q40 idea itself ("passes as a ring") is the right desire.
- "It's my tasbih." as a three-word reveal: short, in-group, lands as the macro appears.
- "Silent tracking" and "60 days money back" are accurate to approved claims; no store metrics, no superlatives, no wudu or reward claims.
- The private-smile close in C03 is the best emotional beat in the asset; build the final line around it.
- Tirmidhi 3375 wording is exact (just reposition).

## Directions for the rewrite
1. **Own the "how others read you" space (differentiate from ad_11).** Keep the meme, make the humour sharper: the coworker's reading of the ring vs the quiet truth. First line should name the scene and tension, e.g. a question that makes the viewer guess what the ring is doing ("Guess what this ring is counting."), with the pill carrying "WHAT MY COWORKERS SEE". Never "my".
2. **Brand-voice or POV, not testimony.** Use captions in a POV register ("POV: the ring in this meeting is a tasbih") or a brand narrator ("It looks like a ring. It counts dhikr."), or second-person scenario ("You think it's a ring..."), so no AI person claims an experience.
3. **Spend the C02 window (4.7 s, about 10 words) on one connected sentence** that says what the ring does in plain verbs: tap, count, feels at 33/66/99, no sound, no screen to open. One sentence, not two noun fragments. Keep "silent tracking" only if exact brand wording is needed.
4. **Give the C03 smile its line, then the offer.** A short human payoff (keeping the tongue moist / dhikr between points, no guilt, no reward claim), then one offer line: $49.99 plus 60-day money-back, with free shipping handled once (pill or VO, not both competing).
5. **Rework the primary text from scratch**: scene-first, one idea, no ad_09/ad_11 phrases, one CTA, hadith (exact, attributed to the Prophet ﷺ) positioned as the human reason, not as the ring's promise; write three headlines that do not echo "a tasbih that looks like a ring". Note any clip changes explicitly: pill 3 text, the flare in C02, the digits on the display, and (optional) a thumb-tap version of C02.
