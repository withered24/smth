1. **Make the split a real contrast.** Left panel = "set down": a phone face-down and a string of beads on the sink edge, wet empty hands reaching in, running tap, no ring in frame (or no counting tool in frame). Right panel = the ring: close-up of a hand under a gentle tap, ring on the **ring finger**, a few droplets on the ring, no submersion, no shower.
2. **Legible count.** Display must read a clean "5332" as in `ad_02_img_v2.jpg` / the product PNG. Vertical or garbled digits (main render) are a fail: the ad is about the ring, and it has to look like the product.
3. **Finger and hand.** The current left hand reads as a man's hand with the ring on a middle-looking finger. Use a woman's hand, or a man's hand with the ring clearly on the ring finger. Never an index or middle finger (Sahih Muslim narration commonly cited against men; the rebuilt store FAQ also needs a flag, see owner list).
4. **Do not depict a finished wudu with the ring untouched.** A hand in the tap with the ring slightly slid up the finger is the safest, most believable picture. No arms-and-face wudu scene.
5. If the owner decides not to re-render: do not run this ad on the old image. The only fallback is a headline-less crop of the right-hand product panel with the new text overlaid, which still needs design time. Recommend the re-render.
