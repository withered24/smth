## Editor notes

### What I changed from the rewrite, and why
1. **Put "wudu" back into the ad.** The rewrite never said the word anywhere in the copy, so the tribe cue was only "sleeves up". The on-image payoff is now "Wudu splashes are fine." and the primary text says "wudu splashes are fine". This is the exact register CONTEXT allows ("IP67 water resistant, so wudu splashes are fine"). It describes the product, not the viewer, so no personal-attribute risk. It is also the only angle ANG11 has to differentiate it from the other ads.
2. **Removed "stays on your finger" and "as you normally would".** "Stays on your finger" sat right next to "slide it aside" and contradicted it. "As you normally would" asserted the viewer's practice (borderline personal-attribute framing). Replaced with a plain instruction: "Slide it aside so water reaches the skin beneath." That matches the fiqh guidance in CONTEXT (move or adjust so water reaches the skin) and never implies wudu is done with the ring untouched.
3. **Named the product category inside the first ~111 characters** (H3 content folded into line 2: "A smart tasbih ring on the finger"). The rewrite's first two lines had no product word, which loses colder viewers before "See more". Line 2 also now literally matches the re-rendered split (phone and beads on the left, ring on the right).
4. **Cut "Something always gets put down."** It was a soft universal claim and the weakest line. The concrete list does the work.
5. **Tightened the mechanism line.** "Then it's back to what it's for: tap to count, and a gentle pulse..." became "Tap to count. A gentle pulse at 33, 66 and 99 tells you where you are." The pulse now has a job, and nothing implies the 100th (Muslim 596a is 33+33+34).
6. **One CTA, one offer line.** Dropped the repeated "try it for 60 days" (it appeared in the offer line and the CTA). Offer line stays once; CTA is "Tap Shop Now."
7. **Headline 3 and description changed** to avoid colliding with other rewrites: "60 days to try it" echoed ad_05 headline 2 ("Built to be kept. 60 days to try.") and ad_09 H4 ("60 days, money back"). Headline 3 is now "$49.99 · 1-year tech warranty"; description carries shipping plus the guarantee so every offer fact appears but not 4 times.
8. **On-image badge 3 "60-DAY GUARANTEE" → "60-DAY MONEY-BACK"** (clearer, matches the offer wording) and badge 2 uses "PULSE AT 33 · 66 · 99" (singular, grammatical). Word counts recounted: on-image 23, primary text 82.
9. **Removed commentary** (why-recommended paragraphs, change tables, re-render prose) from the copy sections; re-render spec moved here.
10. Kept from the rewrite: H1 as the lead hook, the sink scene, IP67 discipline (no "waterproof", no submersion), no testimonial, no store metrics, no reward or iman claim, no office or lost-count drift, no VOC-22 lift.
