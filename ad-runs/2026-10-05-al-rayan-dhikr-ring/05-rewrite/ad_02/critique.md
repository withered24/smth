# ad_02 (image, ANG11 wudu-ready) — Copy critique

Role: copy critic. Current copy = `03-ads/ad_02/copy_final.md`. Creative reviewed: `report_media/ad_02_main.jpg`, `ad_02_img_v2.jpg`, `04-assets/ad_02/images/img_main.png`, `img_v1.png`, plus the swipe (Tayyib lip balm) and the rebuilt store pages.

## 0. One-paragraph verdict

The ad is clean, honest on the product and well-built on the swipe's structure, but it is a weak argument. It sells a feature everyone in the category already has (IP67, Stage 3-4 sophistication, so a copied mechanism) and it invents a pain that does not hold up: "Set a counter down at the sink and you lose your count." A clicker keeps its count, a bead string keeps your place in your hand, so the A1 reader will not recognise this problem, and a fiqh-literate comment section will say so. The hook addresses the viewer as someone who does wudu five times a day, which is a Meta personal-attributes risk. The "split contrast" has no contrast: both panels show the same ring. The ring's display digits are garbled in the render. It needs a truer friction, a situation-first hook, fiqh-safe wudu wording and a re-render of the image.

## 1. Hook strength (would A1/A2 stop scrolling?)

Current ranking (best to worst) for a problem-aware A1 on mobile:

1. **H1 "Wudu five times a day. Your count needs this."** Best of the five because it is a ritual-fact pattern interrupt, and A1 reads it in under a second. Three problems:
   - "Your count needs this" is a payoff with no stated problem. What does the count need? Water protection? A scroller has to decode "this", and the image does not answer it (see section 4).
   - It tells the viewer what they do ("Wudu five times a day") and is written to "your". See section 3 (personal attributes).
   - "Wudu five times a day" is itself a loose claim. Wudu stays valid until broken, so many people make wudu fewer or more than five times. "Five prayers" is the safe fact.
2. **H3 "Lost your count at the sink again?"** The most specific scene and the best emotional hook, but "again" implies a repeated failure on the viewer (negative self-perception framing, mild) and the scene is not credible: nobody loses the count at the sink because of the tool. It stays alive only as a scene ("the sink") with a different problem.
3. **H5 / headline "Wear it to wudu. Keep your count."** Clear benefit, zero tension, no curiosity. It is also the Meta headline AND the CTA line AND the image v1 headline, so it is spent three times. As a headline it is fine. As a hook it is a benefit statement to someone who has not yet felt a problem.
4. **H2 "2-3 counters in your bag. None made for wudu."** Lifts VOC-01 almost word for word ("2-3 manual counters... none are waterproof"), asserts what the viewer owns, and "none made for wudu" is an overreach (beads and finger counting are perfectly fine at wudu). Flabby rhythm.
5. **H4 "Wudu didn't break your count. Your counter did."** Worst. In this audience "break" next to "wudu" reads as nullifying wudu, so it confuses on first read. It blames "your counter", an attack on the viewer's own tool. The premise (anyone thinks wudu breaks the count) is untrue. Nothing in the logic holds.

Net: no existing hook is both a pattern interrupt and true to A1's lived friction. The rewrite needs a new primary hook (see directions).

## 2. Angle, avatar fit, awareness/sophistication, belief order

- **Angle fit.** "Wudu-ready" is a real, concrete A1 worry (VOC-01 "none are waterproof"; conversion barrier "water damage during wudu"). It is a *objection-answering* angle (B5), not a desire angle. Running it as the whole ad means we open on the fear of the product dying, not on the benefit of continuous dhikr. That suits product-aware buyers more than the "problem-aware" label in the brief says.
- **Sophistication.** Stage 3-4 means copied mechanisms are dead on arrival. IP67 is on iQibla, Hadiyah and the store pages of the clones. "Water resistant" alone will not differentiate. The only differentiating move is a specific *moment* (offer_brief: "a specific usage mechanism and moment"). The sink is that moment, but the copy does not use it: it says "wudu" and then jumps to the product spec.
- **Beliefs.** The header says "B4 -> B5" but the body installs B5 first (IP67 / wear it to wudu), then B4 (tap, pulse, silent tracking), and the assignment lists B5, B4. The doc is inconsistent with itself. More important, B5 (built well enough) is proven only by one spec word, and B4 (silent, no phone, no look) arrives as a pile of features, one of which ("discreet tool you can have all the time at work", "silent tracking") belongs to the office angle (ad_06/ad_09/ad_11), not the wudu angle. The primary text drifts from sink to desk.
- **Missing, and cheap to add:** the fiqh-aware beat (what actually happens at wudu), and the one-line permission/Sunnah safety (not necessary on every ad, but this audience reads fiqh in comments; see section 3).

## 3. Rule violations and risks (quote the line)

**A. Meta personal attributes / identity framing (MUST)**
- Hook, on-image headline, primary text line 1 and Meta-style framing: "Wudu five times a day. Your count needs this." Second-person plus a stated religious practice. CONTEXT classes this as borderline, with the preference for situation or product framing. This is the ad's main hook and its on-image headline, so the risk is concentrated on the one line that cannot be hidden. Also "Wudu five times a day. 2-3 counters in your bag." asserts both a practice and ownership of tools.
- `copy_final.md` Avatar line "practising Muslim 20-40" and the spine "A practising Muslim who wudus five times a day and carries 2-3 counters... blames himself when the count breaks" are internal only. Nothing must leak, but note the spine itself builds a "blames himself" negative-self-perception frame, which must not be ported into any line.
- H3 "Lost your count at the sink again?" Mild: "again" implies a recurring personal failing.

**B. Truth / credibility (MUST)**
- "Set a counter down at the sink and you lose your count." Factually weak. A mechanical clicker keeps its count when set down. A counter does not reset because it touched a sink. The only true version: you stop, your hands get busy, and resuming from memory is where the number blurs (that is the real Q29 interruption pain, and it is not a wudu-specific one). A fiqh-literate commenter will point out that nothing about wudu "loses" a bead count.
- "2-3 counters in your bag. None made for wudu." Overreach, see H2.
- "Wudu didn't break your count. Your counter did." Untrue premise, see H4.
- Spec honesty is fine: "IP67 water resistance", no "waterproof", no shower or swim wording anywhere. Keep that.

**C. Wudu fiqh (SHOULD, near-MUST)**
- "Wear it to wudu." (headline, hook H5, primary text twice, CTA line, image v1). CONTEXT allows "can stay on through wudu splashes" and forbids "never take it off" or implying wudu is complete with the ring untouched. The bare imperative "Wear it to wudu" plus "Keep your count" plus the ring shown under running water, untouched, in the left panel, reads as "ring stays on, wudu done". Scholarly guidance is to move or adjust the ring so water reaches the skin beneath (required by some if it is tight). The rebuilt store FAQ gets this right: "washing your hands and splashes during wudu are fine." The ad should borrow that precise register ("splashes", "water resistant") and never state or show a completed wudu with the ring untouched.
- Repeating the phrase 5 times (see C below) makes the absolute reading worse.

**D. Faith content**
- No Quran or hadith is used. That is safe. No attribution errors, no reward-by-ring claim, nothing about the Sunnah of fingers (not required here). Clean.
- Quran 62:10 was optional in the brief. Not needed. Do not bolt it on just to have faith content: "remember Allah often" next to a wudu hook adds nothing.

**E. Men's finger (MUST CHECK, creative)**
- Copy is silent on finger, which is correct. The image hand (left panel) is visibly a man's hand and forearm (hair, build), and the ring sits on a finger in the middle of the hand. From the render it is not clear whether this is the ring finger, and the swipe/prompt says "ring finger". A human must confirm on the full-res frame; if it reads as middle or index it must be re-rendered. The rebuilt store also says "Most people wear it on the index finger", which clashes with the CONTEXT rule if a viewer lands from this ad, so the ad should not show a man's index finger.

**F. Riya', store metrics, banned claims, FTC**
- No counts, no bragging: pass. No store metrics, stars or review counts: pass. No superlatives, no competitor naming: pass. No testimonial, no first-person story: pass (this is a strength vs ad_01/ad_07). No sale price or % off: pass. "Rose gold" not used: pass.
- "A discreet tool you can have all the time at work." is VOC-22 (possibly paraphrased, from one Reddit poster) written as the brand's own promise. CONTEXT says use VOC as the avatar's language, not attributed; this is close to verbatim and also off-angle. Rewrite, do not keep.
- "Silent tracking." is brand wording, allowed. Do not upgrade to "completely silent". It is also not what the ad is about.
- "Tap to count. Feel a gentle pulse at 33, 66 and 99." allowed. Does not imply the 100th; fine.
- The store rebuild includes first-person customer review text (for example about charging twice a week). That is outside this ad, but tell the owner: anyone landing from this ad will see it, and the research has no verified customer quotes.

**G. Repetition inside the ad**
- "Wear it to wudu" appears: Meta headline 1, hook H5, primary text line "Wear it to wudu.", CTA line "wear it to wudu", image v1. Offer facts (60-day guarantee, free US shipping) appear in the badges, the primary text, headline 3 and the description (4 times). Headline 2 "Dhikr ring with IP67 water rating" is a dull spec line and duplicates badge 1.

## 4. Copy-to-creative mismatch

- **No contrast in a "split_contrast" layout.** Swipe logic: left = the problem (cracked lips), right = the product. Here left = hand with the ring on, right = the same ring. There is no problem panel, so "Your count needs this." has no "without this" to land against. The viewer sees two pictures of the solution.
- **"Your count" is not visible.** The ring on the left hand is tiny with an unreadable display ("EES 5"-style garble in `ad_02_main.jpg`; "5332" readable only in `img_v2`/`img_main`). The right-hand hero ring's display renders as stacked or vertical digits (QA already flagged "vertical digits" in the run log and kept it as best attempt). The ad is about the count and the count is the one thing that is unreadable. This is the strongest re-render reason.
- **Water on the ring is untouched.** The left panel shows water pouring over the back of the hand with the ring in place, no ring adjustment, which is the wudu-fiqh point in C. A hand mid-wudu shows rubbing between the fingers and the ring moved, which is also a more believable picture.
- **Text hierarchy.** The black caps line and the green serif line read well. The green line is the largest and the one most skipped ("needs this" is the payoff of a thought that has not been stated). Badge row: two of four badges are offer facts (guarantee, shipping) that also sit in the primary text; "TAP TO COUNT" is the least ownable product fact on the page. "IP67 WATER RESISTANT" in `img_main.png` shows smeared letters (the shipped `ad_02_main.jpg` is clean, so make sure the clean one is what runs).
- Headline-only mismatch: image v1 says "Wear it to wudu. Keep your count." but the recommended hook (H1) is the main image. Two different on-image messages for the same ad, which will split performance data.

## 5. Clarity, rhythm, flab, clichés, overlap

- **Primary text.** Line 1 is a stack of three fragments with three different ideas (ritual, tools, wet hands). The second paragraph is the false claim. The third and fourth carry the product. The fifth is the off-angle office line. The sixth is the offer. It drifts and the "wudu" thread is dropped after paragraph 3.
- **Rhythm.** The short, parallel fragments are good ("Tap to count. Feel a gentle pulse at 33, 66 and 99."). "None made for wet hands." is the best line in the ad and should survive in some form.
- **Flab.** "The Al Rayan Dhikr Ring 2.0 has IP67 water resistance." is a spec sentence in the middle of a pain story. "Tap Shop Now and wear it to wudu." is cute but the third use of the phrase.
- **Clichés.** "needs this" is straight from the swipe (and every lip-balm ad). Acceptable on-image as the swipe structure, but not as the only idea.
- **Overlap with the other 10 ads.**
  - "Wear it to wudu / IP67 so wear it to wudu" also appears in ad_03 (bullet) and ad_10 (line). ad_02 should own it; the others can keep a plain IP67 line (note for orchestrator).
  - "Silent tracking, discreet tool at work" overlaps ad_06, ad_09, ad_11 (office). Remove from ad_02.
  - Lost-count / interruption framing is owned by ad_10, ad_05, ad_07 and ad_01. ad_02 must not pivot into it, which is exactly what the "set a counter down" paragraph does.
  - "Your dhikr was never weak / blame the tool" is used in ad_01, ad_09, ad_10. ad_02 uses a mild version via H4; drop it.
  - Hook shape "X. Y. Your ___ needs this." is unique in the set. Keep the structure only if the hook is rewritten to be situation-based.

## 6. Prioritised list

### MUST FIX
1. Remove the identity assertion from the hook and on-image headline: "Wudu five times a day. Your count needs this." Rewrite so it names a situation or the product, not the viewer's practice (for example five prayers / the sink / wet hands).
2. Remove or replace the untrue pain: "Set a counter down at the sink and you lose your count.", "None made for wudu." and the whole of H4. Replace with a friction that is true: hands wet and busy, the tool must be put down and picked up, the count lives in your head or in a device you cannot touch with wet hands, a ring on the finger keeps the number in sight and the 33/66/99 pulse tells you where you are.
3. Fiqh-safe wudu wording everywhere (hook, headline, primary text, CTA line): "stays on through the splashes" / "built for wet hands", never "wear it to wudu" as an absolute and never a picture or line suggesting wudu is done with the ring untouched. One short, plain line that does not preach is enough (the store FAQ's register).
4. Replace the VOC-22 lift ("A discreet tool you can have all the time at work.") and delete the office drift; it belongs to other ads.
5. Image fix (clip/re-render): display digits on the right ring are garbled/vertical and the left ring display is unreadable; the ad is about the count, so the count must be legible. Confirm the finger the ring is on in the left panel (man's hand, must not be index/middle). Either re-render or say explicitly that on-image changes are not worth it and keep the current clean `ad_02_main.jpg`; if kept, the copy must not depend on the digits.

### SHOULD FIX
6. Make the split an actual contrast (left: the wet-hands problem, for example beads or a clicker set aside on the sink edge or a phone screen-down, hands wet and empty-handed; right: the ring) or drop the split.
7. Cut the "wear it to wudu" repetition to one placement; one headline, one different CTA line.
8. Replace "Wudu five times a day" with a safe fact ("Five prayers a day. Five trips to the sink." or similar) in whatever line survives.
9. Replace the dull Meta headline 2 ("Dhikr ring with IP67 water rating") and de-duplicate the offer lines (badges + primary + headline 3 + description say the same two facts).
10. Fix the beliefs header (B5 then B4 as written, or reorder) and let the copy install the order it claims.
11. Swap badge 2 "TAP TO COUNT" for something more ownable and true ("33 · 66 · 99 pulses", "Silent tracking") if a re-render happens.
12. Tell the orchestrator: ad_03 and ad_10 should drop the "wear it to wudu" line so ad_02 owns it.

### KEEP (what works)
- The ritual-callout structure and the cream/green/black layout from the swipe; the clean typography and bold hierarchy of `ad_02_main.jpg`.
- "None made for wet hands." (the best sentence; the right tone for the hook if reworked).
- The IP67 discipline: "water resistant", no waterproof, no shower/swim, no submerged hands.
- 33/66/99 pulse line, offer line ($49.99, free US shipping, 60-day money-back), "Shop Now".
- No testimonials, no counts, no metrics, no faith overreach, no ring-earns-reward language.
- Short sentences and the plain, warm vocabulary (wudu, dhikr).

## 7. Directions for the rewrite (3-5)

1. **Own the moment, not the spec: "the sink is where the count is most at risk of getting put down."** Hands busy and wet, phone/clicker/beads have to be set aside, tasbih stops mid-number. The ring stays on the finger and keeps the number. Hook candidates (no identity assertion): "Hands wet. Count still there." / "The count stays on your finger. Even at the sink." / "Five prayers a day. Five trips to the sink." Primary text lands on the true sentence: IP67 water resistant, built for wet hands and wudu splashes.
2. **A situation-first scene in second person, not "you do wudu".** "Sleeves up. Tap running. Where were you at, 61 or 71?" resolves into "the ring still shows it." It is a relatable scenario (CONTEXT option b), not a testimonial, and it drops the "counter set down" falsehood by moving the pain to the dhikr in progress.
3. **Fiqh-literate micro-line, once.** "IP67 water resistant: splashes at the sink are fine." Pair it with a line that does not claim wudu is complete in place (optionally "slide it aside when you wash, as you normally would"). This pre-empts the comment-section pedant and builds trust with exactly the audience that reads fiqh.
4. **Give the pulse a job at the sink.** After the wet-hands scene, "a gentle pulse at 33, 66 and 99 tells you where you are without looking" does the B4 work and ties the wudu angle to the product mechanism, instead of a feature list. Keep it within what the brief allows (do not imply the 100th, do not add reward).
5. **Differentiate from ad_03/05/09/10.** Don't use "blame your tools", "lost count" as the headline, or "quiet office". Own: the sink, wet hands, "before salah", "Five prayers", with one CTA that is not the headline repeated ("Tap Shop Now. 60 days to try it."). If the image is re-rendered: left = empty wet hands with a clicker or beads set aside on the sink edge, right = the ring with a legible count, the ring finger (not index/middle) on the left hand, or a woman's hand to avoid the men's-finger question altogether.
