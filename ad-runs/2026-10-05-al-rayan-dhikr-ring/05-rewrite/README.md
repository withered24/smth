**Use `final.md`.** Each one ends with "Editor notes" (what changed and why), any re-render needed, and the owner-to-confirm list.

## Owner to confirm before running
- **Al Rayan app is a real branded app** (ad 01; ad 10 only if the old "App sync" label is kept).
- **Works without your phone** (ad 04, the sentence "Nothing to unlock.").
- **Count is kept when you pause or are interrupted** (ad 10, one line plus hook H5).
- **Size range**: the ads say 16–22 mm, but the rebuilt store lists only 18 / 20 / 22 mm. Make them agree (ad 03).
- **Free size exchanges** (ad 03).
If a claim is not confirmed, delete the tagged sentence. Each `final.md` says exactly what to cut.
