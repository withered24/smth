# ad_10 copy critique (COPY CRITIC, rewrite round)

Ad: image, 9:16, rose-gold ring levitating over a limestone block. Headline "Lost your dhikr count?" with four dotted-leader labels and a green 60-day bar. Swipe: Calm Collective "A skin picker?". Angle ANG3 (never lose count again). Avatar A1. Stage 3-4. Beliefs B2 then B4.

Read for this critique: CONTEXT.md, intake, user market research, avatar sheet, master avatar research, necessary beliefs, offer brief, ad_10 brief, copy_final (identical to copy_chiefed_v1), chief notes, the swipe analysis and image, `report_media/ad_10_main.jpg`, the rebuilt store pages, and the hooks and primary text of the other 10 ads. Note: ad_10 has no entry in `_assign.json` (8 entries only) or `concept_slate.md`. It was an extension round, so the brief and `run_log.md` are the record.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The structure is clean and the truth-hygiene is better than most of the batch: no fabricated testimonial, no store metrics, no men's-finger visual, no reward claim. The problem is that the ad never delivers on its own hook. The headline asks "Lost your dhikr count?", but neither the image labels nor the primary text say the one thing that answers it: **the count is shown on the ring's display and stays on the ring**. The copy lists four generic, copied features (tap, pulse, IP67, app). It then spends its only persuasion line on a thesis ("your tools made it hard") that four other ads already run. The hook is also near-duplicated in at least four sibling ads. Fixable without re-rendering, except the weakest label.

## 1. Hook strength

Context: the primary text sits above the image in feed, so the scroller meets the primary-text line first, then the image headline. The first screen is two things at once.

Ranking of the five existing hooks for A1/A2:

| Rank | Hook | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | H1 "Lost your dhikr count?" | Best for this image. Four words, readable at thumbnail, the word "dhikr" does the identity work in under a second (chief's note N1 was right), and it is the situation-framing CONTEXT.md names as preferred. Weakness: it is a generic functional question with no scene and no stakes. "Count" is mundane. Almost every dhikr-counter ad could open this way. Overlaps ad_02 H3 ("Lost your count at the sink again?"), ad_05 H5 ("Still losing count?"), ad_09 H4 ("Lost your count on the beads. Again."). |
| 2 | H4 "Tap. Pulse at 33. Keep going." | Best rhythm in the set, and the only hook that is about what the ring does. But it is a feature hook, not the assigned lost-count angle, and it echoes ad_07 H1/H5 and ad_11 CALLOUT_2 ("Feel 33, 66, 99."). Usable as a secondary or headline-field line. |
| 3 | H2 "Kids interrupted. Count gone." | The only hook with a scene and a consequence, so it is emotionally the strongest. Costs: assumes a parent (soft personal-attribute / relevance narrowing), and the scene is already owned by ad_07 H2 ("I lost my count every time my kids interrupted") and ad_05 H3 ("One bump, and the count is gone"). Fragment-y but it works. |
| 4 | H5 "Lost count again?" | Near-clone of H1 and of ad_05 H5. "Again" nags and edges toward the "you + negative self-perception" line. Drop. |
| 5 | H3 "Stop counting in your head." | Worst. Imperative, bossy, and does not match the lost-count angle. A tribe-literate reader can also hear it as a dig at counting with the heart or fingers, which sits close to the Sunnah finger-counting sensitivity (Abu Dawud 1501, VOC-07/36). Drop. |

Would A1/A2 stop? A1 who already counts high volumes (A1b) would pause on H1 for a beat. A cold A1 would not, because nothing in the frame shows loss or an interruption: a still, pretty ring. The Calm Collective swipe worked because "A skin picker?" is a specific, slightly embarrassing behaviour; "lost count" is the equivalent but is undersold. A2 (gift buyer) has no reason to stop. That is fine, this is an A1 ad.

Primary-text line 1 repeats the image headline verbatim ("Lost your dhikr count? One interruption and the beads, or your head, start over."). In feed those are inches apart: the same hook twice, and the second one wastes the first ~125 characters (the only guaranteed-visible text). Line 1 should add a new beat, not echo.

## 2. Angle, avatar fit, awareness, beliefs

- Angle/avatar fit: ANG3 for A1 is on-evidence (Q29, Q02, Q01). Good choice. But the execution is a feature list wearing a pain headline.
- Mechanism gap (the big one): the lost-count promise is only credible if the copy says the ring **shows and holds the count**. Primary text never mentions the display, never says the count stays when you stop, and never says you can look down and see where you are. It says "puts the count on your finger" (metaphor) then lists tap, pulse, IP67, app. The pulse at 33/66/99 is a milestone buzz; it does not tell you "47". The display does. A sophisticated A1 who has read about Q11 clones will notice the copy never explains how the ring solves the actual problem.
- Image labels do not carry the angle either. Four labels: "Tap to count" / "Pulse at 33-66-99" / "IP67 for wudu" / "App sync". None says "keeps your count". The image headline asks a question the image never answers.
- Awareness/sophistication: stage 3-4 means copied features are not enough; you need a specific mechanism or moment. The copy only has the generic claims every competitor makes (tap, vibration, IP67, app sync). The specific moment (mid-set interruption) is gestured at in line 2 but not dramatised.
- Beliefs in order:
  - B2 (friction, not intention): asserted in one line ("Your dhikr was never weak. Your tools made it hard.") after a 3-item list of interruptions. Installed thinly. Also see the flag on this line in section 3.
  - B4 (a ring can count silently, without a phone/noise/screen): half installed. Tap, pulse, app are there. "Silent tracking" (brand wording, allowed) is missing, as is "looks like a ring" and the small display. For an ad whose B4 is the destination, "silent" and "display" are the load-bearing words.
  - B3 (counting aids permitted; fingers remain Sunnah) was not assigned, and nothing here conflicts with it. But a comment-section audience that counts on fingers (Q07, Q36) is the exact crowd reading a "lost count" ad. A single honouring line would defuse the top predictable comment.
- Trust: the only risk-reversal is the 60-day guarantee. A1's real fear (Q11, clone died in a month) is served by adding the 1-year tech warranty, which is allowed and unused.

## 4. Copy-to-creative mismatch (what the image shows)

What `ad_10_main.jpg` actually shows: top-centre "Al Rayan" wordmark, headline "Lost your dhikr count?" in heavy black sans, one rose-gold ring floating at an angle over a cream stone, display reads 5332 in one row, gold logo button left of the display, four green labels on dotted leaders on the right, a green bar "60-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE" across the stone, and a large empty stone area below.

- Hook-to-image: nothing depicts loss, interruption or continuity. The picture is calm and generic. The headline carries all the meaning and the labels do not pay it off.
- Primary text vs image: primary text says "kids, a colleague, a bump". The image has no people or scene. That is normal for this swipe structure, but it means the scene must be done in words, and the current words are three clipped phrases.
- Display is the star and is not mentioned: the 5332 display is the biggest proof object in the image, and neither the labels nor the primary text point to it ("small display shows your count" is never said). Missed opportunity: the leader for "Tap to count" ends near the display, so it could say "Count stays on the display" with no re-render of the headline.
- "Pulse at 33-66-99": hyphens make it read like a range or a score. Primary text writes "33, 66 and 99". Minor inconsistency; comma version reads cleaner.
- "App sync": weakest of the four and does not support the headline.
- Leaders: labels 3 and 4 have dotted leaders that end near the lower band, not at a feature of the ring (there is no "feature" there to point to). Cosmetic only.
- Bar placement: bar sits slightly lower than the spec and the bottom quarter is empty stone; harmless, and the empty area helps with Meta's bottom UI overlay. No change needed.
- Faith/adab on the image: no hand, no finger, no verse. Clean. Colour is rose gold, consistent with the brief.
- On-image text quality: headline excellent (size, weight, contrast). Labels are legible but small and mid-weight green on cream; fine on a phone but they carry the least weight in the layout, which is where the angle should have landed.
- Re-render verdict: a single re-render swapping label 4 ("App sync") for a lost-count payoff such as "Count stays on the ring" is clearly worth it, because it is the only way the image answers its own headline. Everything else can be fixed in text. Label 3 and the hyphens could ride along on the same re-render. Tag the retention label `[CONFIRM: count retained on the ring]` in the notes.

## 5. Clarity, rhythm, flab, clichés, overlap

- Line 1 duplicates the headline (see section 1). Line 1's second sentence "One interruption and the beads, or your head, start over." is clumsy: a subject that is "beads, or your head" breaks the rhythm and parallelism.
- Line 2, three clipped fragments ("The kids walk in. A colleague talks. Someone bumps your arm."): the rhythm is good, the content is generic and used elsewhere. It names the interruption but never the cost (the "47 or 74? start over?" moment is the real pain, and nobody has written it).
- Line 3 is the cliché of the whole batch. "Your dhikr was never weak. Your tools made it hard." is a store-headline derivative ("blame your tools") and appears in a near-identical form in ad_01 H3, ad_09 H3, ad_05 line 1 and ad_04 H4. Context says use it at most where it is the angle. For ad_10 the angle is lost count, not tool-blame.
- Lines 4-6 are a flat feature roll-call: "Tap to count. A gentle pulse at 33, 66 and 99." / "IP67 water resistance, so wear it to wudu. It syncs with the Al Rayan app." No "so what". Each feature should be tied to the moment: tap, glance, keep going.
- "Tap" is used for the ring action and for the CTA ("Tap Shop Now and keep your count"). A light echo that works, but "Tap to count... Tap Shop Now" is repeated verbatim in ad_04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 11. It is the house CTA, not a flaw, but it adds to sameness.
- The offer line repeats the bar and Meta description (60-day guarantee appears three times). The warranty is unused.
- Meta headlines: #1 "Keep your dhikr count on your finger" is serviceable but "on your finger" is vague. #2 "Tap to count. Pulse at 33, 66, 99." duplicates the on-image labels and echoes ad_05 H1/ad_07/ad_11 headlines. #3 is a price/product tag (fine as a fallback, no emotion).
- Meta description "60-day money-back guarantee" merely repeats the bar. Free shipping or warranty would add information.

Overlap with the other 10 ads (the most important diversity problem):

| Ad_10 element | Also appears in |
|---|---|
| "Lost your dhikr count?" / "Lost count again?" | ad_02 H3, ad_05 H5 ("Still losing count?"), ad_09 H4 |
| "Kids interrupted. Count gone." / "The kids walk in" | ad_07 H2 and primary text, ad_05 line 2 and H3 |
| "Your dhikr was never weak. Your tools made it hard." | ad_01 H3, ad_09 H3/primary text, ad_05 line 1, ad_04 H4 |
| "Tap to count. Pulse at 33, 66, 99" as headline | ad_05, ad_07, ad_11 headline fields |
| Feature roll-call (tap/pulse/IP67/app) | ad_02, 03, 04, 05, 11 primary texts |

Net: ad_10 is the least distinct ad in the batch. Its unique ownable territory should be the mid-set moment (where you were in the count), the display, and the "pick up where you left off" payoff.

## 6. Prioritised fix list

### MUST FIX
1. Change "so wear it to wudu" to a wudu-safe formulation ("IP67 water resistant, so wudu splashes are fine" or "stays on through wudu splashes"). No "wear it to wudu", no "never take it off".
2. Remove or reframe "Your dhikr was never weak. Your tools made it hard." It plants a weak-dhikr self-image (guilt/negative-self-perception risk) and duplicates four ads. Do not replace it with another blame-your-tools line.
3. Close the mechanism gap: say that the small display shows the count and that the count stays on the ring. Tag the retention claim `[CONFIRM: count is retained on the ring when you stop or are interrupted]`. Without it, the angle has no mechanism.
4. Stop the primary text from repeating the image headline in line 1. Open with a new, specific beat (the mid-set moment).
5. Differentiate from ad_02/05/07/09: drop "kids", drop "again", drop the "tools" thesis, avoid "one bump, count gone". Pick a scene none of them uses.

### SHOULD FIX
6. Add the missing B4 words: "silent tracking" (brand wording, allowed) and, if useful, "looks like a ring" (visible fact). No "completely silent", no "works without your phone" unless tagged.
7. Tag or drop "It syncs with the Al Rayan app" (`[CONFIRM: Al Rayan app is live and branded]`); swap on-image label 4 "App sync" for a lost-count payoff (needs a re-render, worth it, see section 4).
8. Neutralise the interruption cast (doorbell, a call, a knock) so it does not assume parenthood and does not collide with ad_07.
9. Add one honouring line for Sunnah finger counting so the ring reads as a helper for long counts, and so the fiqh-literate commenter has nothing to fight.
10. Use the 1-year tech warranty (allowed) next to the 60-day guarantee for the A1 clone-fear (Q11). Replace the Meta description "60-day money-back guarantee" with free shipping or warranty.
11. Replace "33-66-99" with "33, 66, 99" if the image is re-rendered; reword "IP67 for wudu" to "IP67 water resistant" in the same pass.
12. Rewrite headline options so none collides with ad_05/07/11 ("Tap to count. Pulse at 33, 66, 99" is already taken).

### KEEP (what works)
- Image headline "Lost your dhikr count?": four words, readable at thumbnail, identity word present, CONTEXT.md-approved framing. Keep it on the image (no re-render needed).
- The swipe structure: brand wordmark, big question, levitating rose-gold ring, dotted-leader labels, bar. It is legible and premium.
- Ring fidelity: single-row 5332 display, gold logo button, rose gold only, no hand or finger. Correct.
- Truth hygiene: no testimonial, no stats, no stars, no superlatives, no competitor mentions, no weight/IP68/battery/sale price. The 596a-safe "33, 66 and 99" phrasing.
- The 2nd-person scenario format for the interruptions (acceptable under rule (b)). Short fragment rhythm in line 2.
- The offer line facts ($49.99, free US shipping, 60-day money-back) and the one-CTA close.
- No guilt tone in the headline and no explicit shaming.

## 7. Concrete directions for the rewrite

(Examples are directions, not final copy. All second-person scenario or product voice, nothing attributed to a customer.)

1. **Own the mid-set moment.** Open the primary text inside the count: "You were somewhere past 40. Then the phone rang. Now it's 47, or maybe 74." Then the turn: "A small display on the ring shows the number." This is the specific moment, unclaimed by ads 02/05/07/09, and it needs no kids. Keep the image headline unchanged so there is no re-render for the hook.
2. **Make the display and the 33/66/99 pulse do different jobs.** Display: "Look down, there's your number." Pulse: "A gentle pulse marks 33, 66 and 99." Together they are the stage-4 mechanism the copy currently lacks. Tag the retention line `[CONFIRM: count stays on the ring]` and keep "silent tracking" in brand wording.
3. **Swap the tools-blame thesis for a pick-up-where-you-left-off payoff.** Calm, no guilt: "Interrupted? Your count is still there." Pair with "silent tracking" and a "looks like a ring" line to complete B4. No "weak", no "your tools".
4. **Respect and wudu in one tight trust beat.** "Fingers stay the Sunnah; the ring holds the long counts." Then "IP67 water resistant, so wudu splashes are fine." Add the 1-year tech warranty next to the 60-day guarantee. Optional faith anchor if the owner wants one: Quran 33:41, exact wording "O you who have believed, remember Allah with much remembrance", cited, not used elsewhere in the batch; never tie reward to the ring.
5. **Rework the Meta headline set around the moment, not the specs.** Directions: a "never lose your place" line, a "pick up where you left off" line, and a clean product-and-price fallback. Make each under 40 characters, none repeating "Pulse at 33, 66, 99". If one re-render is allowed, replace on-image label 4 "App sync" with the count-retention payoff `[CONFIRM]` and fix "33-66-99" and "IP67 for wudu" in the same pass.
