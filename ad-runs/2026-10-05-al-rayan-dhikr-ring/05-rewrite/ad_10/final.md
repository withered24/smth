1. **Opener rebuilt to avoid ad_07.** The rewrite's "You come back. 2. **Retention claim now isolated.** The unconfirmed "count stays on the ring" appeared in the image label, two hooks, a Meta headline and the CTA. It now lives in one tagged primary-text line, one tagged hook (H5) and one tagged alt headline.
