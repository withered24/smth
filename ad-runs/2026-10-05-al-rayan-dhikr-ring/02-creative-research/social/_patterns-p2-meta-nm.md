## Formats / visual patterns
- Jewelry/ring image ads are mostly white-background packshots + price (Mejuri 2284206889070575, Calm Collective 1393387235676220, Casual Carats 1025485556665592, Alpine 1127192782953198, Corinthian 1402828511722527): weak first glance, run long on retargeting budgets, not swipe-worthy.
- Winners with structure: oversized headline + old-vs-new split + seal + offer bar (Carbinox); hero panel + glow macro collage (A Force For Good); warm hands-only lifestyle photo with 3-bullet offer stack in copy (Aydins); yellow price banner + inset detail bubble (Christian Divinity 2089060295022686).
- Native UI spoof: Notes app (1641454606915417).
- Typical image sizes: 4:5 and 9:16 stories; library previews are 600px.

## Visual styles
- Dark carbon + gold bar (Carbinox); warm wood/latte natural light (Aydins); cool pale blue + black macro with glow (A Force For Good); white seamless packshots (most jewelry).
- Product placement: tiny on hand in lifestyle; hero scale in comparison/banner ads.
