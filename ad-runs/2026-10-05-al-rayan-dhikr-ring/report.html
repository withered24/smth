What to check before launch

  • Ads 09 to 11 (extension round) passed visual QA on the first render, 2 credits each, no variations rendered. No scouted non-Muslim ad met the S-tier bar outright; the three strongest structures (A-tier, 66 to 241 days live) were used, with their star ratings, customer counts and lifetime claims stripped out.
  • Ring display: the tiny four-digit screen is the hardest detail for the image model. Ads 02 and 03 show the digits running along the band after two attempts; ad_08 C03 is soft on close crop. Look at these before spending on them.
  • Of 15 image variations, 8 passed QA and appear below their main image. The other 7 (wrong or garbled ring digits, text in the CTA band) were left out. Variations get no retries.
  • ad_06 voice-over was sped up 1.15x to fit the 11-second meme pacing.
  • Store spec conflicts (weight 7.7 g vs 7.2 g, IP67 vs IP68 on the homepage, a $22.65 sale price, battery life) were kept out of every ad. Only IP67 (product page), $49.99, free US shipping, the 60-day guarantee and the 1-year warranty are used. Confirm IP67 before running ad_02.
  • "Silent tracking" in ad_06 is the brand's own wording from the product page; the ads never say "completely silent".
  • Voices were chosen by preset name (no audition): ad_06 uses Maya; ads 07 and 08 use the warm, calm female presets picked to the scripts.
  • Wave-1 copy workers stopped on an API spend limit (429) and were restarted after the reset. No credits were lost.
  • Reddit MCP was offline and the forum scout was skipped at your request; the VOC comes from your research doc.
